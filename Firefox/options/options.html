FindReplace withAaWhole

Capitalisation follows the page: synergy becomes hot air, Synergy becomes Hot air, SYNERGY becomes HOT AIR. Tick Aa to match only the casing you typed, untick Whole to match inside longer words. Separate several replacements with commas and they take turns down the page — with hot air, word salad, 💨 the first one becomes hot air, the next word salad, and so on.
