FindReplace withAaWhole

Capitalisation follows the page: trump becomes pig, Trump becomes Pig, TRUMP becomes PIG. Tick Aa to match only the casing you typed, untick Whole to match inside longer words. Separate several replacements with commas — each page picks one at random, so pig, clown, 🤡 keeps things fresh.
